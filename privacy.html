Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Vithhlesh Traders website handles information. The website is a simple information site for our grocery and food-grain business in Piplod, Dahod, Gujarat.

Enquiry form

The enquiry form asks for your name, mobile number, customer type, product interest, quantity and an optional message. These details are not sent to or stored on this website. When you submit the form, your browser prepares a message that opens in WhatsApp (or that you can share with us by phone). You can review the message before choosing to send it.

If you send the message, we receive it on WhatsApp and use it only to respond to your enquiry. WhatsApp is operated by Meta and its own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

Phone calls and maps

Call links open your phone's dialler. Map and directions links open Google Maps, which is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Fonts and hosting

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to operate and secure the website. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete enquiry messages you have sent us by contacting the shop on 098987 70189.
